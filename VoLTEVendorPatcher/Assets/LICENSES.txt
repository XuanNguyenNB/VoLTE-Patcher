* e2fsprogs debugfs/e2fsck (GPL/LGPL/BSD/MIT components as described by the
  upstream NOTICE file). See Tools/e2fsprogs-NOTICE.txt.

  analysis for this tool and is used unchanged. Redistribution must follow
