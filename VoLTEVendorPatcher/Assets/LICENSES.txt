VoLTE Vendor Patcher bundles the following third-party components:

* e2fsprogs debugfs/e2fsck (GPL/LGPL/BSD/MIT components as described by the
  upstream NOTICE file). See Tools/e2fsprogs-NOTICE.txt.
* Cygwin runtime DLL (GNU GPL with Cygwin exception). See
  Tools/Cygwin-COPYING.txt.
* The sample GAQ VoLTE overlay APK is supplied by the device firmware
  analysis for this tool and is used unchanged. Redistribution must follow
  the applicable firmware/OEM terms.
* SharpCompress 0.50.4 (MIT) is used to read RAR archives without invoking
  WinRAR, 7-Zip, PowerShell, or another runtime process.

Native source references and pinned hashes are in Build/tools.lock.json.
The SharpCompress version is pinned in VoLTEVendorPatcher.csproj; its full MIT
license is bundled as Tools/SharpCompress-LICENSE.txt.
